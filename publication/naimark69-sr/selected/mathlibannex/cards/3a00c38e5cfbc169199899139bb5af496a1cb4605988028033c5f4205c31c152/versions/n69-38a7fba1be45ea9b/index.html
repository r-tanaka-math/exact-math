Definition

Choose the automorphisms first and require the four conditions for those choices. Innerness is expressed as existence of an implementing unitary for each index. Norm completeness supplies pointwise limits from the two Cauchy conditions, while the exact inverse identities allow the two limits to be proved inverse to each other.

Assumptions

Only continuity and complex linearity of the two functionals are inputs to this definition. Positivity, normalization, and purity are not required here. The inverse in the second Cauchy condition is the inverse of that same .

Conclusion

The datum is a sequence with four requirements: forward Cauchy behavior, inverse Cauchy behavior, innerness of each term, and pointwise convergence of the transported functional. A limiting automorphism is a later construction, not an additional field of the datum.
