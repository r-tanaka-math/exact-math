The faithful separable representation gives the carrier bound . Independently, the density obstruction gives for every norm-dense . Taking proves that the norm density is exactly and supplies the continuum-density counterexample.

Carrier cardinality, norm-density character and Hilbert-space dimension remain separate notions. The existence statement allows a possibly nonunital algebra and does not include a faithful separable representation as one of its quantified requirements. Its chosen witness happens to be the fixed unital algebra .
