The fixed-left-hand-side limit calculation is retained with its full dependence in the pointed-transport Card.

Pointed transport proves uniqueness among all state extensions. Uniqueness gives invariance under source unitaries; their span and the actual represented shell limits put all generators in the closed centralizer. This proves traciality. A different restriction argument then proves uniqueness among all tracial states.
