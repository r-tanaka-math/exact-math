Transports the irreducible spectrum along a homeomorphism of geometric structure spaces.

Statement

Let be complex C*-algebras, possibly without identities. Suppose

The map determines an ordinary homeomorphism

Assumptions

The source requires nonzero complex C*-algebras , each with its compatible order, and the specified full closure-space homeomorphism . The underlying Hilbert spaces of irreducible representations are not fixed as additional inputs; the independent spectra use their pure-state GNS presentation. The map has domain , whose points are subsets of ; it is not a map and no linear or multiplicative property on algebra elements is assumed.

Conclusion

The output is this specific ordinary homeomorphism between the independent irreducible spectra. It uses the given full closure-space homeomorphism and the two fixed spectral comparisons. The input acts on geometric points, which are subsets of algebras, not on algebra elements or representation operators.
