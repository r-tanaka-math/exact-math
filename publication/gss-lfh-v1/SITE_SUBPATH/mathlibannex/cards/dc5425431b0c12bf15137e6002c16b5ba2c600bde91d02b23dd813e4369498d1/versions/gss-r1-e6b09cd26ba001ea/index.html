Assumptions

The source carries the compatible C*-order on . The Hilbert spaces may have different dimensions, and neither representation is assumed faithful. Each of the four vectors is required to be nonzero. The relation on the left is the pre-existing geometric finite-chain relation, not a relation defined by representation equivalence.

Conclusion

The assertion concerns the full finite-chain relation and one simultaneous intertwiner of the two original representations. Neither direction replaces that relation by direct linking of every pair in a component. Faithfulness is not a conclusion.
