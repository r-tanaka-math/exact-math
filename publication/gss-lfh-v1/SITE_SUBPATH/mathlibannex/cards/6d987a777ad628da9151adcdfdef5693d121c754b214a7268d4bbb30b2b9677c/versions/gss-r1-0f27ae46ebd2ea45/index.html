ClosureSpace.HasContinuousFiniteConjugation

Defines when finite conjugation is continuous in both directions.

Statement

This property requires a conjugate and its inverse to be fully continuous whenever and satisfy the finite-continuity and forward full-continuity conditions below. The requirement ranges over every component of every closure space in a specified class.
