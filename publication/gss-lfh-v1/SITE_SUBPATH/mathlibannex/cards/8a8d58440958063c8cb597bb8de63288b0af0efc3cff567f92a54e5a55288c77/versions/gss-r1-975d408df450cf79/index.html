Identifies an algebra with the c₀-sum of a dense orthogonal family of closed ideals.

Statement

Let be a family of closed -ideals in a complex C*-algebra . Assume whenever , and , and that the set of finite sums of elements of these ideals is norm dense in . The ideal-sum map defines a -isomorphism from the dependent -sum of these ideals onto .

Definition

Here truncation sets coordinates outside the finite set to zero. On a general family, is the continuous extension of finite ideal summation. Its injectivity and surjectivity under density make this same map a bijection. It preserves addition, multiplication, complex scalars and involution.

Assumptions

The family consists of closed -ideals satisfying the stated ordered zero-product condition. Density concerns all finite sums in , not density of each individual ideal. No countability or nonempty-index assumption is imposed.

Conclusion

The output is directed from the ideal -sum to . Its inverse gives the ideal coordinates used in the subsequent compact-operator decomposition.

def c0SumEquivOfDense (hJ : PairwiseOrthogonal J)
    (hdense : Dense (finiteSums J)) :
    C0DirectSum (Fiber J) ≃⋆ₐ[ℂ] A :=
  { Equiv.ofBijective (c0SumHom J hJ)
      ⟨c0SumHom_injective J hJ, c0SumHom_surjective J hJ hdense⟩ with
    map_add' := map_add (c0SumHom J hJ)
    map_mul' := map_mul (c0SumHom J hJ)
    map_smul' := map_smul (c0SumHom J hJ)
    map_star' := map_star (c0SumHom J hJ) }
