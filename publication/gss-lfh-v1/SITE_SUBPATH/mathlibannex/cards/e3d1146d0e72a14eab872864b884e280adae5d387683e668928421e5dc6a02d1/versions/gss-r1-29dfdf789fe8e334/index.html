ClosureSpace.HasContinuousFiniteCompactComparison

def

Requires a finite homeomorphism to a compact-operator geometric space whose forward map is fully continuous.

Statement

Let be a closure space. A continuous finite compact comparison is the existence of a nonzero complex Hilbert space and a finite homeomorphism from to the geometric structure closure space whose forward map is also fully continuous.

Definition

Here denotes the algebra of compact operators on . For a complex normed space , consists of the unions of kernels of norm-one bounded complex-linear functionals supporting each maximal proper face of its closed unit ball. Faces use real convexity. The support-functional norm theorem justifies the norm-one description for these nonempty faces. Its closure is .

For closure spaces , full continuity of means for every . Finite continuity requires this only for finite . The condition holds when there exists a bijection such that and are finitely continuous, and additionally

The norm and inner product belong to the same nonzero Hilbert space . Full continuity of is not required by this condition.

Assumptions

The input is an arbitrary closure space. It need not already be presented as a GSS or an operator-algebra normal part. There is no representation of a C*-algebra in the input or conclusion. The model Hilbert space is nonzero; no separability or finite-dimensionality is required.

Conclusion

This is an existence property of a closure space, with forward full continuity added to finite continuity of a bijection and its inverse. No representation range condition is part of this definition.
