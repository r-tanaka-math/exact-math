Definition

The source is the irreducible spectrum: pure norm-one positive bounded complex-linear functionals on , modulo unitary equivalence of their GNS representations restricted to . Equality of representation kernels is not the equivalence relation. Its topology is induced by the kernel map into the primitive spectrum with hull-kernel topology.

Assumptions

The algebra is nonzero and has the compatible C*-order required by the pure-state and primitive-kernel constructions. The ordinary topology on is the kernel-induced topology just described; no topology from a quotient of the pure-state space is substituted. No injectivity of is imposed.

Conclusion

The output is the specified ordinary homeomorphism with action (1), for the kernel-induced topology on and the stated topology on . Its inverse is the inverse of this same bijection. No injectivity of the kernel map into the primitive spectrum is asserted.

def irreducibleSpectrumHomeomorph :
    @_root_.Homeomorph (IrreducibleSpectrum A) (NormedSpace.GeometricSpectrum ℂ A)
      inferInstance (geometricSpectrumTopology A) :=
  (irreducibleSpectrumClosureHomeomorph A).toTopologicalHomeomorph
    (isTopologizable_irreducibleSpectrumClosure A) (isTopologizable_geometricSpectrumClosure A)
