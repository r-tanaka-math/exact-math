ClosureSpace.FiniteHomeomorph

structure

Packages one bijection together with finite continuity of the map and its actual inverse.

Statement

Let and be closure spaces. A finite-homeomorphism from to is a bijection whose forward map and actual inverse both preserve closure on finite test subsets. It is a structure carrying the map and these two properties, rather than only a proposition asserting that some map exists.

Definition

For a function and a subset of its domain, write . A finite-homeomorphism consists of a bijection , with its inverse , satisfying

and

The inverse in the second inclusion belongs to the same bijection: and . The first inclusion transports every point of the closure of a finite source set into the closure of its image. The second imposes the corresponding requirement in the reverse direction.

Here “finite” qualifies the test sets and . The sets , the image , and the closures need not be finite. The closure operations themselves still act on all subsets.

Assumptions

The two given closure operations are extensive, monotone and idempotent and preserve the empty set. The carriers may be empty and may belong to independent universes. No topology, metric, dimension bound or finite-character property of either closure is an input.

Full continuity would require the first inclusion for every subset of , not only finite ones, and similarly for the inverse on . Neither full-continuity requirement is a field of this structure.

Conclusion

An element of the defined type supplies one invertible map with both finite-continuity properties. These data can be used wherever a finite-homeomorphism is required without supplying ordinary continuity.

The separately defined continuous finite-homeomorphism adds full continuity of the forward map; even that additional structure does not include inverse full continuity. This distinction concerns an extra field, not a change to the underlying closure operators.

def Continuous (c : ClosureSpace α) (d : ClosureSpace β) (f : α → β) : Prop :=
  ∀ s : Set α, f '' c.closure s ⊆ d.closure (f '' s)

/-- Preservation of closure, tested only on finite subsets of the domain. -/
def FinitelyContinuous (c : ClosureSpace α) (d : ClosureSpace β) (f : α → β) : Prop :=
  ∀ s : Set α, s.Finite → f '' c.closure s ⊆ d.closure (f '' s)
