Assumptions

The bases are arbitrary complete orthonormal families. They need not be countable, and neither space is assumed separable. No infinite-dimensionality, same-type condition on two projective factors, or full continuity of is assumed. Dimensions one and two are included through the finite-dimensional branch. The inclusion representations are nonzero and irreducible, and the algebras are norm closed; unitality is not added.

Conclusion

Forward full continuity preserves Hilbert-basis index cardinality, without assuming full continuity of the inverse. The conclusion neither chooses a basis-to-basis map nor asserts an isometry.
