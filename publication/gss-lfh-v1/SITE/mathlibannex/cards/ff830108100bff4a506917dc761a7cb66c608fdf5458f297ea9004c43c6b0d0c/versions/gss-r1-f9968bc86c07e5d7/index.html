Assumptions

Maximality is with respect to inclusion, among pairwise-linked subsets of the same normal part. There is no hypothesis that is closed, finite, or initially nonempty. The nonzero-action hypothesis excludes the zero Hilbert space. Dimension one is allowed; no lower bound of two is added.

Conclusion

Every maximal family fixes either the source projective line or the target projective line, and every fiber in (1) is maximal. The two descriptions need not be different: in dimension one both fibers are the whole singleton normal part. No uniqueness between the two alternatives of (2) is asserted.
