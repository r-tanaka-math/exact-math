MathlibAnnex.Analysis.CStarAlgebra.CompactOperatorC0Sum

abbrev

Names the c₀-sum of a family of compact-operator algebras.

Statement

Let be any family of complete complex Hilbert spaces. This abbreviation denotes the -sum of the compact-operator algebras .

Definition

Use the dependent -sum with fiber , the compact operators on the given . Its elements are bounded families , , such that for every only finitely many have . Its norm is and its algebra operations are coordinatewise. The RHS specifies precisely this family of subalgebras of bounded operators.

Assumptions

Every has its own normed additive group, complex inner product and completeness. Zero and infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces are allowed. There is no restriction on the size of the index set.

Conclusion

The output is an abbreviation for this dependent algebra. It does not denote all compact operators on a Hilbert-space sum and does not include additional off-diagonal operator entries.
