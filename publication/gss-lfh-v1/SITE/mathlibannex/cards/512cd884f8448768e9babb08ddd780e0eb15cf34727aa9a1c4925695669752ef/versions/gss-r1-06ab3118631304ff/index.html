Obtains algebraic rigidity from an actual full bounded-operator model on either side.

Statement

Let be complex C*-algebras. For a complex C*-algebra , write for its geometric structure space. The equality is required for every subset, not just finite subsets.

Assumptions

The bounded-operator model is required on at least one side. It is an isomorphism onto all of , not onto a proper subalgebra. The Hilbert space is complex and complete; it and the algebras may be zero. No nonzero, unitality, separability, countability, or finite-dimensionality hypothesis is imposed.

Conclusion

The equivalence concerns existence of a -algebra isomorphism and existence of a closure-space homeomorphism. It does not require the isomorphism to induce a prescribed homeomorphism, or assert uniqueness.
