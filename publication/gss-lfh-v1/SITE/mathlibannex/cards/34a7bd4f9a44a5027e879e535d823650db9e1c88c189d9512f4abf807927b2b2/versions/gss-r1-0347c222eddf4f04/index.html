Assumptions

Faces use the underlying real convex structure, while every functional and every kernel in (1) is complex-linear on the full algebra . The source uses the compatible C*-order. Nontriviality excludes the zero algebra, where the original face family is empty but the dual ball has the extreme point zero. No change to the original definition of is part of the assertion.

Conclusion

Formula (1) is equality of families of subsets, with both inclusions. Repeated kernels represent one point. No injectivity of the kernel parametrization, and no equality between a functional kernel and a representation kernel, is asserted.
