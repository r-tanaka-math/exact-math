MathlibAnnex.Analysis.CStarAlgebra.IrreducibleSpectrum

def

Defines the irreducible spectrum using unitary equivalence classes of pure-state GNS representations.

Statement

Let be a complex C*-algebra with compatible order, not necessarily unital or nonzero. The irreducible spectrum here is the quotient of its pure states by unitary equivalence of their GNS representations of .

Definition

A pure state is a positive norm-one bounded complex-linear functional , extreme among such functionals for real convexity. Let denote the GNS representation of its canonical extension to the forced unitization, restricted to the original algebra . The pure-state GNS irreducibility theorem guarantees irreducibility of this action.

Write when and are unitarily equivalent: there is a surjective complex-linear isometry between their Hilbert spaces such that for every . The definition is

The quotient uses this unitary-equivalence relation. Equality of representation kernels is not substituted for unitary equivalence. The definition assigns no topology; the later ordinary spectrum topology is induced through the representation-kernel map, not specified here as a pure-state quotient topology.

Assumptions

Only the complex C*-algebra and its compatible order are required. The set of pure states may be empty. No auxiliary Hilbert space or separability assumption is required.

Conclusion

The result is the set of unitary equivalence classes of pure-state GNS representations. The definition permits an empty quotient when there are no pure-state representatives.
