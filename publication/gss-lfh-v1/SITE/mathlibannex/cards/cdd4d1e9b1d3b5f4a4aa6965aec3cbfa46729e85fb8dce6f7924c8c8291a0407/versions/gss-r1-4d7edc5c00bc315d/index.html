Assumptions

The normal-part bijection and its inverse satisfy finite continuity. The spaces are complex Hilbert spaces, and the two norm-closed -subalgebras act nontrivially and irreducibly. The algebraic dimension of is at least three; it may be infinite.

The two projective factors come from the same factorization of . Their semilinear lifts may have different scalar automorphisms. Neither those automorphisms nor the lifts are assumed continuous or bounded.

Conclusion

One of the alternatives (3) and (4) holds for every nonzero pair of vectors, in both directions. These are equalities of points of the normal parts and hence of their coefficient kernels. The orientation is fixed globally, and the formulas use the same and their inverses throughout. Continuity on arbitrary subsets and boundedness of the lifts are not conclusions of this theorem.
