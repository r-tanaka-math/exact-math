Derives scalar regularity and bounded invertibility from closed images of closed hyperplanes.

Assumptions

Here a closed complex hyperplane is a closed complex-linear subspace of codimension one. The assumption requires its image under to be closed.

Both and are Banach spaces; only is required to be infinite dimensional. No separability, Hilbert-space structure, or initial continuity or boundedness of , , or is assumed.

Conclusion

The given semilinear equivalence is complex-linear or conjugate-linear and is boundedly invertible. The conclusion includes continuity of and and one pair of positive constants satisfying both estimates (2). It does not require those constants to be optimal or equal.
