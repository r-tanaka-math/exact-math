theorem

Obtains algebraic rigidity when either side admits a faithful compact action.

Statement

Let be complex C*-algebras. For a complex C*-algebra , write for its geometric structure space. Its points are the unions of kernels of norm-one bounded complex-linear functionals supporting a maximal proper face of the closed unit ball of ; faces use real convexity. Write for its hull–kernel closure:

These are the geometric structure definition and its closure, with the norm-one description justified by the norm-one support characterization. Let denote the algebra of compact operators on a complex Hilbert space .

Assume that at least one of admits a faithful -representation on a complex Hilbert space whose image is contained in . Then and are -isomorphic if and only if and are homeomorphic as closure spaces. Here a closure-space homeomorphism is a bijection satisfying

The equality is required for every subset, not just finite subsets.

Assumptions

A faithful compact-valued representation is required on at least one side. All Hilbert spaces are complex and complete. The algebras and representation spaces may be zero. Representations need not be unital; no separability or countability assumption is imposed.

Conclusion

The equivalence concerns existence of a -algebra isomorphism and existence of a closure-space homeomorphism. It does not require the isomorphism to induce a prescribed homeomorphism, or assert uniqueness.

Main citations

The either-side compact rigidity theorem is the stated equivalence. Rigidity for a given compact action allows an independent Hilbert universe and uses the canonical compact c0-decomposition. The existential representation comparison links the existential condition to the same reduced atomic action.
