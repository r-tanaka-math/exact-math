Glyph supplement for Naimark20 private staging, prepared 2026-09-24.

The existing four fixed font assets remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The
U+1D520 MATHEMATICAL FRAKTUR SMALL C glyph is supplied by unmodified
FreeSerif.ttf from GNU FreeFont 20120503, the same GNU FreeFont release family
as the existing FreeMono n-ary supplement. No primary typeface, mathematical
character, renderer family, or layout template is replaced.
