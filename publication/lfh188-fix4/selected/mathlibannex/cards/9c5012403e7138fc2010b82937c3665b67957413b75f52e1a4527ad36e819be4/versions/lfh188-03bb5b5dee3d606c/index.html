MathlibAnnex.Topology.exists_isOpen_singleton

theorem

Applies the Baire property to the closed cover by singletons.

Statement

Let be a nonempty countable topological space which is and Baire. Then some point is isolated: its singleton is open.

Assumptions

The condition says that singletons are closed. No metric, completeness, compactness or Hausdorff assumption beyond is required.

Conclusion

At least one singleton is open; the conclusion does not say that every point is isolated.
