∃! φ ranges over continuous functionals on ShellFamilyTarget family; the predicate includes state-space membership and the identity for all a b. Lean realization notes

Faithfulness of a representation and faithfulness of this state are different assertions. This theorem asserts existence and uniqueness of a tracial state. The separate result traceExtension_star_mul_self_eq_zero_iff concerns state faithfulness and is not an additional hypothesis here.
