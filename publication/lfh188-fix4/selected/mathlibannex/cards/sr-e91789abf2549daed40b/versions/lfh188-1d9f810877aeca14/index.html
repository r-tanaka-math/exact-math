Conclusion

One admissible frame attains the maximum of the absolute determinant. The numerical maximum depends on , although its value does not depend on which maximizer is chosen. Positivity is a separate result.

Proof route

Use compactness of the finite product of dual balls and continuity of the absolute determinant.
