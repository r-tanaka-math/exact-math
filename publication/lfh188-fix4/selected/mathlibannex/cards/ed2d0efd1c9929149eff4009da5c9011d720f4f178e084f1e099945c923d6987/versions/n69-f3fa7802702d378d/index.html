Lean realization notes

The declaration defines the continuous extension. Positivity, normalization and traciality are separate proved results, not conclusions established merely by giving the definition. Uniqueness among all normalized continuous traces is proved in the CAR trace-uniqueness theorem. No statement about extending this trace to the larger shell-generated target is part of this construction.
