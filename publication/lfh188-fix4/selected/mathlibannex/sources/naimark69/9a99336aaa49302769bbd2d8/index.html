5/-!
6# A source-parametric atomic model from projection shells
7
8This theorem consumes only representation-local source data: pairwise
9inequivalent irreducible fibers, selected unit vectors, decreasing normalized
10flags, and shell partial isometries with their exact initial/final supports.
11It constructs the off-diagonal rank-one completions, proves their termwise
12shell relations, and proves irreducibility of the displayed concrete target.
13No target capture, target simplicity, or final Naimark conclusion is a field
14or hypothesis.
15-/

72      (S + InnerProductSpace.rankOne ℂ
73          (coordinateEmbedding o (xi o)) (coordinateEmbedding i (xi i))) ∈

75      (S + InnerProductSpace.rankOne ℂ
76          (coordinateEmbedding o (xi o)) (coordinateEmbedding i (xi i)))
77          (coordinateEmbedding i (xi i)) = coordinateEmbedding o (xi o) ∧

79          (coordinateEmbedding o (xi o)) (coordinateEmbedding i (xi i))).comp
80          (Submodule.projectionShell (U i) n) = W i n := by
