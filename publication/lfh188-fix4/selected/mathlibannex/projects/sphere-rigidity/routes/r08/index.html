Scope

The absolute Jacobian integral of the radial map yields target-ball volume. A limiting linear contraction has the exact determinant-volume scaling between the two unit balls. Ball inclusion and strict compact-subset volume comparison give ball equality; the separate ball-equality result then gives norm preservation.
